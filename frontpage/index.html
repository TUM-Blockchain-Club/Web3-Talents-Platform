How a week works

Every topic runs through the same four stages, so you always know where you are.

01

Expert Input

An industry expert introduces the topic through a live lecture.

02

Become a Specialist

In small peer groups you research, discuss and prepare a presentation on a subtopic.

03

Teach your Peers

You present your findings to other groups, and they teach you theirs.

04

Expert Validation

A second expert lecture connects the pieces and deepens your understanding.
